Why Millrod Swim Academy?

Professional instruction with a personal touch.

Welcome to Millrod Swim Academy. We are Noam and Rivka Millrod, a husband-and-wife swim instruction team dedicated to providing professional, high-quality swimming lessons.

With more than 30 years of combined teaching experience, we have helped children and adults develop confidence, improve their swimming abilities, and build lifelong water safety skills.

Our lessons are designed for every level — from beginners learning their first strokes to experienced swimmers looking to improve technique, endurance, and efficiency.
